Crafting Interpreters – Robert Nystrom
📌 Free online

Virtual Machines – Iain D. Craig

3️⃣ Compilers (actual compiler, not proofs)
✅ Primary
Engineering a Compiler – Cooper & Torczon
• This is the anti-Dragon Book
• Focused on: 
• IRs
• Optimization passes
• Real compiler pipelines
• You can actually implement what it describes
✅ Practical supplement
Modern Compiler Implementation in C – Andrew Appel
• Project-based
• Very close to “write a compiler by following chapters”
• C-friendly, not Java-heavy nonsense
📌 End result:
You can write a real optimizing compiler, not just a parser.
4️⃣ Electronics (build real hardware + PCBs)
You want to design, not memorize Ohm’s law for 300 pages.
✅ Foundation
Practical Electronics for Inventors – Paul Scherz
• This is big, but: 
• Extremely practical
• Covers components → circuits → systems
• You’ll actually understand what parts to use and why
✅ PCB + modern practice
The Art of Electronics – Horowitz & Hill
(+ optional Learning the Art of Electronics workbook)
• Industry standard
• Teaches: 
• Signal integrity
• Power design
• Analog/digital crossover
• Makes you dangerous (in a good way)
📌 Pair this with KiCad and you’re designing PCBs confidently.
5️⃣ Microprocessors / CPU organization
You want how CPUs actually work, not marketing slides.
✅ Best overall
Computer Organization and Design (RISC-V Edition) – Patterson & Hennessy
• Explains: 
• Pipelines
• Caches
• Memory hierarchy
• RISC-V makes things clean and understandable
✅ Hardcore option
Structured Computer Organization – Andrew Tanenbaum
• Bottom-up
• From gates → CPU → OS boundary
• Very good for mental models
📌 This connects directly to OS + compiler work.
